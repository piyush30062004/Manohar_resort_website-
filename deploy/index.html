Our story

A real family home, not a resort built for show.

The Khot family has lived on this island for over four hundred years.

Manohar Resort Homestay sits on Khot Juva island in Kalavali, Malvan taluka, Sindhudurg, with the Gad River on every side. It's a working homestead first. Anil Khot and his family host you in the same rhythm the island has kept for generations.

It's quiet, remote enough to feel like an escape, and your meals come from the family kitchen rather than off a restaurant menu. Guests keep calling it a "home away from home", and that's exactly what it is.

On location · Famous faces on our island

Ankita Walawalkar & Abhijeet Sawant came here to shoot.

Abhijeet Sawant, the singer who won the very first season of Indian Idol, and Ankita Walawalkar brought their camera crew to Khot Juva. They filmed out on the Gad River in a wooden boat, and in the village, outside a red-tiled Konkan home.

Both clips were shot on the day. The same river and the same lanes are right outside your door.

A day on Khot Juva

From noon light to firelight.

Seven moments from one ordinary day on the island. Scroll, and watch the sun cross the sky.

On the islandMidday
01 / 07
  2. 01 Middayआकाश

    Looking straight up

    Lie back under the trees. Green on every side, open sky in the middle, and only birds for traffic.

  3. 02 Late afternoonबगळे

    Egrets in the shallows

    The mudflats draw a small crowd of egrets as the light turns from white to gold. Five minutes' walk from the rooms.

  4. 03 Golden hourभातशेती

    Gold over the paddy

    Coconut palms lean over the rice fields while the sun drops toward the river.

  5. 04 Sunsetसूर्यास्त

    Evening over the backwater

    The palms on the far bank go dark first, and the water holds the colour a little longer.

  6. 05 Duskहोडी

    The family boat

    Tied up at the jetty once the day's fishing and ferrying is done, the way the island has always used the river.

  7. 06 After darkनदीकाठ

    Dinner on the water's edge

    The pavilion lights come up, the river goes still, and the whole place is doubled in the reflection.

  8. 07 By the fireशेकोटी

    Fireside evenings

    Stories and laughter with the family and whoever else is staying that week, and no hurry to go to bed.
