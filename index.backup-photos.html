Our story

A real family home, not a resort built for show.

The Khot family has lived on this island for over four hundred years.

Manohar Resort Homestay sits on Khot Juva island in Kalavali, Malvan taluka, Sindhudurg, with the Gad River on every side. It's a working homestead first. Anil Khot and his family host you in the same rhythm the island has kept for generations.

It's quiet, remote enough to feel like an escape, and your meals come from the family kitchen rather than off a restaurant menu. Guests keep calling it a "home away from home", and that's exactly what it is.
